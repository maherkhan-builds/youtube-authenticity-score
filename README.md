The Problem

Viewers often struggle to assess the credibility of YouTube videos and channels. With AI-generated content, sensational headlines, deepfakes, and algorithm-driven engagement tactics, it has become harder to distinguish trustworthy content from misleading or low-quality information.

Trust is eroding — and viewers lack structured tools to evaluate authenticity objectively.

The Solution

YouTube Authenticity Score is an AI-powered analysis app that evaluates credibility signals in videos and channels and generates a structured, logic-validated trust score.

Instead of vague opinions, the app analyzes measurable authenticity indicators and produces a transparent breakdown.

Core Capabilities

Content Signal Analysis – Evaluates title, description, claims, and tone

Channel Credibility Assessment – Reviews channel history, consistency, and content patterns

Engagement Pattern Review – Detects suspicious spikes or inorganic activity

Claim Risk Evaluation – Flags exaggerated, unverifiable, or high-risk claims

Authenticity Score (0–100) – Generates a structured, weighted trust score with explanation

Why This Requires a Purpose-Built App

General AI tools can summarize or comment on content, but authenticity evaluation requires structured scoring logic, consistent signal weighting, and repeatable validation workflows.

This app:

Applies consistent evaluation criteria across videos

Uses weighted signal categories

Generates explainable scores

Validates logical consistency before output

It transforms subjective impressions into a structured credibility framework — something conversational tools alone are not designed to systematically manage.

Core Evaluation Framework
Step 1: Signal Collection

Metadata extraction (title, tags, description)

Claim density detection

Tone analysis (sensational vs neutral)

Channel history signals

Engagement anomaly detection

Step 2: Weighted Scoring Model
Category	Weight
Claim Verifiability	25%
Channel History Consistency	20%
Engagement Authenticity	20%
Sensational Language Risk	15%
Transparency Signals	20%
Step 3: Logical Validation

Cross-check score alignment

Detect scoring inconsistencies

Adjust for signal conflicts

Output:

Final Authenticity Score (0–100)

Confidence Level (Low / Medium / High)

Risk Summary

Clear Explanation Breakdown

Authenticity Score: 74 / 100
